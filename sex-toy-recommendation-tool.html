How to Choose the Right Toy

Finding the right toy can feel overwhelming with so many options out there, but it really comes down to understanding your own body and what kind of sensation you crave. A good place to start is thinking about what already feels good during masturbation or sex—do you prefer broad pressure, targeted buzzing, or internal fullness?

It's completely normal to buy a toy that ends up not being your favorite. Treat it as an educational experience to learn more about your sexual blueprint. If you're a beginner, start with something simple like a bullet or a basic wand before investing in complex, dual-stimulation devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know what sex toy to buy?

Start by identifying whether you prefer internal or external stimulation. If you're unsure, external clitoral stimulation (like a small bullet vibrator or a wand) is the most common preference for vulva owners. Our Sex Toy Finder quiz above is designed to help narrow down your options based on your specific desires.

What is the best vibrator for beginners?

For beginners, small bullet vibrators or simple external wands are excellent choices. They are easy to control, not overly intimidating, and affordable. They allow you to figure out if you prefer rumbly or buzzy sensations without a large investment.

Are cheap sex toys safe to use?

It depends on the material. Toys made from "jelly," TPR, or TPE are porous and can harbor bacteria, even with washing. It's always best to invest in 100% medical-grade silicone, ABS plastic, or glass, which are body-safe, non-porous, and easy to clean.

How do I introduce a sex toy to my partner?

Communication is key. Bring it up outside the bedroom first, keeping the conversation positive. Emphasize that a toy is an addition to your intimate life, not a replacement for your partner. You can even shop for one together to make it a shared experience.

How should I clean and store my adult toys?

Wash your silicone toys with warm water and mild, unscented soap before and after every use, or use a specialized antibacterial toy cleaner. Ensure they are completely dry before storing them in a cool, dry place. Keep silicone toys from touching each other in storage, as they can degrade or melt together.

Safety & Care Tips

Material Matters: Always look for 100% body-safe silicone, ABS plastic, or glass. Avoid "jelly" toys as they are porous and can harbor bacteria.

Lubrication: Use water-based lube with silicone toys. Silicone lube can degrade silicone toys over time.

Cleaning: Clean your toys with mild soap and warm water or a specialized toy cleaner before and after every use.

Where to Buy: We recommend shopping at reputable, sex-positive retailers like Good Vibrations, Spectrum Boutique, or directly from manufacturers (like LELO or Womanizer). Avoid third-party marketplace sellers to ensure you're getting genuine, body-safe products.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does stress affect my libido?

Stress triggers the release of cortisol, which can directly suppress sexual hormones like testosterone. High stress also keeps your body in a "fight or flight" mode, making it difficult to feel relaxed and open to intimacy. Tracking both can help you see this correlation clearly.

Does my menstrual cycle impact sexual energy?

Absolutely. For people who menstruate, hormonal fluctuations throughout the cycle (like spikes in estrogen and testosterone during ovulation) can naturally increase libido. Conversely, drops in hormones during the luteal phase may lower desire and energy.

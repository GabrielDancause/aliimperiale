Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sexual Personality Type?
Your sexual personality type is a unique combination of how you communicate desires, experience arousal, seek emotional connection, and approach physical intimacy. Understanding it can help you communicate better with partners and advocate for your needs.
Can my sexual style change over time?
Absolutely! Human sexuality is fluid. Your sexual style can evolve based on life experiences, relationship dynamics, aging, stress levels, and personal growth. It's normal for your preferences to shift.
What if my partner and I have different styles?
Having different styles is incredibly common and can actually be a source of growth and excitement. The key is communication, mutual respect, and finding creative ways to bridge the gap so both partners feel fulfilled.
Is any sexual style 'better' than others?
No. All styles are valid and healthy as long as they involve enthusiastic, informed consent between adults. The goal of this quiz is self-discovery and empowerment, not judgment or ranking.
How should I use these results?
Use your results as a conversation starter! Reflect on whether the description resonates with you, share it with your partner, and use the compatibility insights to foster deeper discussions about your intimate life.

Understanding Enthusiastic Consent

Consent is an ongoing agreement to participate in sexual activity. It's not just the absence of a "no"; it's the presence of an enthusiastic "yes."

The FRIES Model of Consent (Planned Parenthood)

  • F Freely Given: Without pressure, force, manipulation, or while drunk/high.
  • R Reversible: Anyone can change their mind at any time, even if you've done it before.
  • I Informed: You can only consent if you have the full story (e.g., knowing if a partner is using protection).
  • E Enthusiastic: It should be about doing things you WANT to do, not things you feel expected to do.
  • S Specific: Saying yes to one act (like kissing) doesn't mean saying yes to others.

Boundary Negotiation Guide

Discussing boundaries can feel intimidating, but it's the foundation of incredible, safe intimacy. Here are tips for negotiating:

  • Timing is Everything: Discuss boundaries before getting into bed or getting turned on. A neutral setting (like over coffee) takes the pressure off.
  • Use "I" Statements: "I feel most comfortable when..." or "I need..." instead of "You should..."
  • Embrace the Awkwardness: It's okay to feel silly or blush. Acknowledge it: "I feel a bit nervous bringing this up, but it's important to me."
  • Rejection Sensitivity: If a partner sets a boundary, remember it's about their needs, not a rejection of you.
  • The "Traffic Light" System: Green (Go ahead), Yellow (Slow down/Check in), Red (Stop immediately).

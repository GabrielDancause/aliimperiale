Frequently Asked Questions

What is sexual chemistry?

Sexual chemistry goes beyond physical attraction. It's the complex interplay of emotional connection, communication styles, fantasy alignment, and mutual desire that creates intense mutual attraction and satisfying intimacy between partners.

Can sexual compatibility be improved?

Absolutely. While initial sparks are largely spontaneous, long-term sexual chemistry requires effort, open communication, and a willingness to explore each other's evolving desires. Our tool provides targeted exercises to help build this connection.

Is this assessment medically diagnostic?

No, this tool is strictly educational. It is designed to spark self-discovery and facilitate communication between partners based on relationship psychology principles, not to diagnose medical or psychological conditions.

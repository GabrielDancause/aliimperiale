Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Sexual Fantasy Exploration Wheel?

The Sexual Fantasy Exploration Wheel is an interactive desire discovery tool designed to help you safely explore, categorize, and understand your sexual imagination. It provides randomized categories, detailed descriptions, and communication scripts to guide you through various themes.

How can this fantasy wheel improve my intimate life?

Using a sexual imagination guide like this wheel helps normalize your desires. Many people feel shame around their fantasies simply because they don't understand them. This tool provides structured ways to differentiate between what you want in reality versus fantasy, and offers practical scripts for discussing fantasies with partners, ultimately improving communication and intimacy.

Do I have to act out the fantasies I discover?

Absolutely not. A key part of sexual fantasy exploration is understanding the difference between enjoying a scenario in your imagination versus wanting to experience it in reality. The tool explicitly helps you categorize desires as "fantasy only" or "reality potential," reinforcing that our minds are a safe playground.

Is my exploration history private?

Yes. Your exploration history is saved using your browser's local storage (`localStorage`). This means the data never leaves your device and is not sent to any servers. If you share a device, you can use the "Clear All History" button to remove your saved results.

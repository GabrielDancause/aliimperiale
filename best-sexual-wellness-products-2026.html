Sex Toy Buying Guide & Sexual Wellness Resources

Navigating the world of sexual wellness products can be overwhelming. This guide is designed to help you make informed, safe, and satisfying choices for your body and pleasure.

Material Safety (The Gold Standard)

Always look for products made from body-safe, non-porous materials. Porous materials (like jelly, rubber, or PVC) can harbor bacteria and are not recommended.

  • Medical-Grade Silicone: The industry gold standard. It's non-porous, hypoallergenic, boilable, and holds temperature well. (Note: Do not use silicone lube with silicone toys).
  • Borosilicate Glass: Completely non-porous, excellent for temperature play (can be warmed or cooled), and very easy to clean.
  • ABS Plastic/Metal: Hard materials that are safe and transmit vibrations intensely.

Choosing the Right Lube

Lubricant is the most essential sexual wellness product, but not all lubes are created equal. Match your lube to your activity and toys:

  • Water-Based: Safe with all toys, easy to clean, best for sensitive skin. Can dry out and require reapplication.
  • Silicone-Based: Long-lasting, excellent for water play. Never use with silicone toys. Harder to wash off.
  • Oil-Based: Great for massage and masturbation. Never use with latex barriers as it will degrade them.

Where to Buy Safely

When purchasing sexual wellness products, especially hardware like vibrators or ingestibles like supplements, where you buy is just as important as what you buy.

  • Direct from Manufacturer: The safest route to ensure you are getting an authentic product with a valid warranty (e.g., buying directly from Womanizer or Lovense).
  • Reputable Specialized Retailers: Sites like Spectrum Boutique, Babeland, Good Vibrations, or Early to Bed thoroughly vet their inventory for safety and body-safe materials.
  • Avoid Unverified Third-Party Sellers: Be extremely cautious buying toys on large general marketplaces like Amazon. Counterfeit toys made from unsafe, toxic materials are prevalent.

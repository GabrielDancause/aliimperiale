Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a book "sex-positive"?

A sex-positive book approaches sexuality with an open, non-judgmental, and inclusive perspective. It emphasizes consent, pleasure, diversity, and views consensual sexual expression as a healthy, natural part of human life rather than something shameful.

Are these books suitable for beginners?

Yes! We've intentionally curated a mix of approachable, beginner-friendly texts alongside deep dives. Use the "Reading Level" filter above to find books that match where you're currently at in your journey.

Do you use affiliate links?

No. In the spirit of keeping things honest and transparent, none of the links in this directory are affiliate links. These are genuine recommendations based on their educational value and impact.

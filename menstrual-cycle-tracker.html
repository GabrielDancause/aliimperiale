Menstrual Health & Awareness

Understanding your cycle is a key part of sexual and reproductive wellness. By tracking your periods and symptoms, you can better understand your body's natural rhythms and identify any irregularities.

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) Awareness

PCOS is a common hormonal disorder that can affect fertility and cause irregular periods, excess androgen levels, and polycystic ovaries. Tracking your cycle can help identify potential signs of PCOS, such as consistently irregular or missed periods. If you notice significant irregularities, consider discussing them with a healthcare provider.

Endometriosis Awareness

Endometriosis is a condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus, causing pain and sometimes infertility. Severe menstrual cramps (dysmenorrhea) that disrupt daily life, chronic pelvic pain, and pain during intercourse can be symptoms. Tracking the severity of your pain and symptoms can provide valuable information for your doctor.

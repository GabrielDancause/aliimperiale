Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this relationship compatibility quiz?

This quiz uses a weighted scoring algorithm across six key dimensions (Communication, Conflict Resolution, Intimacy, Life Goals, Values, and Emotional Needs) designed by relationship experts. While it provides deep insights and a solid framework for discussion, it is for educational purposes and should not replace professional relationship counseling.

Should I take this quiz alone or with my partner?

You can do both! Taking it alone helps clarify your own needs and perceptions of the relationship. Taking it separately and then comparing your printable results is a fantastic way to spark meaningful conversations about areas where your perspectives align or differ.

What happens if we get a low compatibility score?

A lower score doesn't mean your relationship is doomed! It simply highlights areas where you may need to invest more effort, communication, or compromise. The personalized results provide specific advice and discussion prompts to help you bridge those gaps.

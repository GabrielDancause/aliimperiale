Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a sex education program "inclusive"?

Inclusive sex education goes beyond cisgender and heterosexual norms. It acknowledges and celebrates diverse sexual orientations, gender identities, bodies, and relationships. It includes comprehensive information on LGBTQ+ sexual health, pleasure-based education, and culturally responsive approaches.

Are these programs suitable for schools?

Yes, our directory includes specific filters for "School Curricula" which highlight programs designed to meet or exceed national standards while providing comprehensive, inclusive content suitable for various age groups.

Can I get certified as a sex educator through these programs?

Many of the programs listed under our "Certification Programs" category offer formal training and pathways to certification through recognized bodies like AASECT (American Association of Sexuality Educators, Counselors and Therapists).

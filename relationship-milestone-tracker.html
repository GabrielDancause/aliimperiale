Why We Should Set Intentions Together

Hey, I'm Ali. If there's one thing I've learned from talking to thousands of couples (and through my own trial and error), it's that great relationships don't just happen on autopilot. When we first get together, we're naturally focused on each other. But as life gets busy, it's so easy to slip into the "roommate phase" where you're just managing schedules instead of building a life.

That's where intentional goal-setting comes in. It's not about treating your relationship like a corporate project—it's about making sure you're both rowing the boat in the same direction and actually taking time to celebrate the wins.

Types of Milestones to Track

When we think of "relationship milestones," we usually picture the big stuff: moving in, getting engaged, having kids. But honestly? The smaller, everyday milestones are what actually build the foundation.

  • Communication: Having your first big fight and actually resolving it without yelling. Going to couples therapy. Establishing a weekly "state of the union" check-in.
  • Intimacy: Trying a new fantasy together. Scheduling sex and actually looking forward to it. Learning each other's erotic blueprints.
  • Finances: Having the awkward money talk. Opening a joint savings account for a trip. Paying off a shared debt.
  • Just for Fun: Cooking a complicated meal together without snapping at each other. Finally finishing that TV series. Taking a completely unplugged weekend trip.

How to Use This Tracker

Frequently Asked Questions

What are important relationship milestones?
Important relationship milestones go beyond just moving in together or getting married. They include having your first big disagreement and resolving it healthily, discussing finances openly, taking your first trip together, and feeling comfortable being fully vulnerable with each other.
How do we set relationship goals together?
Set relationship goals by scheduling a dedicated, relaxed time to talk. Start by discussing what is going well, then share your personal and shared visions for the future. Use a tracker to set actionable goals across categories like communication, intimacy, and finances.
Why is tracking relationship milestones important?
Tracking relationship milestones helps couples stay intentional about their growth. It provides opportunities to celebrate small wins, ensures both partners are aligned on their future direction, and helps prioritize the relationship amidst busy daily life.
What are some fun relationship goal ideas?
Fun relationship goals could include planning a surprise date night once a month, taking a class together (like cooking or dance), reading the same book, or saving up for a specific adventure or getaway.

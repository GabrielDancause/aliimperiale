How it works

This quiz is designed to help you build a personalized "Intimacy Profile" spanning multiple categories:

  • Intimacy Styles: How you naturally connect and express affection.
  • Communication: Your preferred methods for discussing desires and boundaries.
  • Boundaries: Your comfort levels with different types of exploration.
  • Kink Interests: Areas of curiosity and established preferences.
  • Romantic Languages: What makes you feel most loved and desired.
  • Physical Preferences: Dynamics of physical touch and connection.

Take this quiz individually and share your results with your partner, or take it side-by-side to spark meaningful conversations. There are no right or wrong answers—only opportunities to understand yourself and each other better.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is sexual compatibility important?

Sexual compatibility isn't about perfectly matching desires from day one. It's about being willing to communicate, adapt, and explore together. Understanding each other's intimate styles builds trust, reduces friction, and enhances overall relationship satisfaction.

What should we do if our quiz results are very different?

Different results are completely normal and an opportunity for growth! Use the conversation starters in your profiles to discuss how you can meet in the middle. Often, mismatched libidos or styles can be bridged with intentional communication and scheduled intimacy check-ins.

How often should couples retake this quiz?

Desires, boundaries, and preferences change over time due to stress, life stages, hormones, and personal growth. It's healthy to check in on your sexual compatibility every 6 to 12 months, or whenever you feel disconnected.

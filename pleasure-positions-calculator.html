Frequently Asked Questions

How does the positions calculator work?

The calculator filters a curated database of educational positions based on your physical preferences, flexibility, accessibility needs, and what kind of intimacy you're looking for. Simply check the boxes on the left to narrow down the results to what works best for your body and your goals.

Are these positions safe during pregnancy?

Yes, we include a specific filter for "Pregnancy Friendly" positions, which generally avoid deep penetration, laying flat on the back (after the first trimester), and undue pressure on the belly. However, every pregnancy is different, so always consult with your healthcare provider about what's safe for your specific situation.

Is this tool inclusive for all bodies?

Absolutely. We specifically designed filters for various body types (including plus-size friendly options), flexibility levels, and specific accessibility considerations (like knee or back pain, and limited mobility) to ensure everyone can find positions that work comfortably for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a sexual pleasure personality type?

A pleasure personality type categorizes the core ways you prefer to experience arousal, intimacy, and satisfaction. While everyone is unique, finding your dominant type helps you understand what naturally turns you on—whether it's physical touch, emotional connection, novelty, or sensory experiences.

How can knowing my pleasure type improve my sex life?

Understanding your type gives you a roadmap to what feels best for you. It empowers you to communicate your needs clearly to partners, explore activities that genuinely excite you, and avoid frustrating encounters that don't align with your core desires.

Can my pleasure personality type change?

Absolutely. Your desires are fluid and can evolve over time based on your life stage, relationship dynamics, stress levels, and personal growth. It's totally normal for a highly Physical type to lean more Emotional with a certain partner or at a different point in life.

What if I fit into more than one type?

Most people are a blend of types! You likely have a primary type and one or two secondary types. The goal isn't to put yourself in a box, but rather to give you a vocabulary to describe the multifaceted ways you enjoy pleasure.

Understanding Your Patterns

Tracking your arousal and pleasure isn't about hitting "perfect" scores—it's about gathering data on your own unique body and mind. We often treat our sexual responses as mysterious, unpredictable forces, but they are deeply connected to our overall well-being.

The Context Matters

Notice how your stress levels and sleep quality impact your desire and pleasure. The "brakes and accelerators" model of sexual response shows us that it's often more about removing the brakes (like stress and exhaustion) than hitting the accelerator.

Cycle Awareness

If you menstruate, your hormonal fluctuations significantly impact your natural arousal baseline. Many people experience a peak in desire around ovulation (when estrogen and testosterone are high) and a dip during the luteal phase (when progesterone dominates). Tracking helps you work with your cycle rather than fighting it.

Privacy & Security FAQ

Is my data private and secure?

Yes, absolutely. This tool relies entirely on local storage within your browser. No personal data, arousal tracking, or pleasure patterns ever leave your device or are sent to any servers. It stays right here on your phone or computer.

How do I save my tracking data?

Your tracking data is automatically saved to your browser's local storage as you log entries. You can also use the Export feature to download your records as a CSV or JSON file to keep a permanent backup.

Can I use this tool as a couple?

Yes! You can choose to track individually or collaboratively with a partner to understand mutual pleasure patterns and communication effectiveness.

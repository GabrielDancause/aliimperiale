Frequently Asked Questions

What is body positivity?

Body positivity is a social movement rooted in the belief that all human beings should have a positive body image, while challenging the ways in which society presents and views the physical body. The movement advocates for the acceptance of all bodies regardless of physical ability, size, gender, race, or appearance.

Who are some popular body-positive influencers to follow?

There are many incredible body-positive influencers creating amazing content. Some well-known advocates include Megan Jayne Crabbe (@meganjaynecrabbe), Spencer Barbosa (@spencer.barbosa), and Alicia Mccarvell (@aliciamccarvell), who all champion self-love, mental health, and diverse representation across platforms like Instagram and TikTok.

How does body positivity relate to sexual wellness?

Body positivity is deeply connected to sexual wellness. Feeling comfortable and confident in your own skin allows for more open communication, better boundary setting, and a greater capacity to experience and advocate for your own pleasure without shame.

What's the difference between body positivity and body neutrality?

While body positivity focuses on loving your body regardless of its appearance, body neutrality aims to decouple self-worth from physical appearance entirely. Body neutrality focuses on appreciating what your body can do rather than what it looks like, offering a resting place for those who find constant body love exhausting.

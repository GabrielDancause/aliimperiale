Consent Scenarios Training Tool

An interactive, judgment-free guide to navigating enthusiastic consent, setting boundaries, and communicating clearly in the real world.

Hey, it's Ali! Consent isn't just a legal checkbox or a buzzkill—it's the foundation of incredible, trusting, and fun experiences. But let's be real, real-life situations can be messy, nuanced, and totally confusing.

I built this training tool to help you practice navigating tricky scenarios across dating, the workplace, and intimate relationships. We'll explore what enthusiastic consent actually looks like, how to set boundaries like a pro, and how to communicate when things get awkward. Your progress is saved automatically, so feel free to take your time.

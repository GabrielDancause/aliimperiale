15 Best Sex-Positive Podcasts

Whether you're looking to spice up your relationship, understand your body better, or just listen to some juicy stories, these are the top sex-positive podcasts you need to be listening to right now.

#1 Overall

Sex With Emily

Hosted by Dr. Emily Morse

A staple in the sex-positive community. Dr. Emily Morse answers listener questions, interviews experts, and talks openly about everything from orgasms and communication to kinks and dating.

Target Audience: Anyone looking for practical, science-backed sex and relationship advice.
Why It's Unique: Dr. Emily's background as a doctor of human sexuality makes this an incredibly educational yet deeply relatable listen.
Episode Highlight: "The Big O: Reclaiming Your Orgasm" — A fantastic breakdown of anatomy and mental blocks preventing climax.
Education Advice
#2 Best Banter

Shameless Sex

Hosted by Amy Baldwin and April Lampert

Amy and April bring humor, real-life experience, and incredible expert guests to the table. They cover topics that usually carry a lot of stigma and discuss them openly and shamelessly.

Target Audience: Those who want sex education delivered with the vibe of two best friends having a glass of wine.
Why It's Unique: Their dynamic makes even the most intimidating topics feel approachable and fun.
Episode Highlight: "Shameless Flirting & Seduction" — Practical tips on how to build tension and flirt naturally.
Education Comedy
#3 Best Interviews

Doing It! with Hannah Witton

Hosted by Hannah Witton

Hannah sits down with different guests each week to talk about sex, relationships, dating, and bodies. It's a wonderful, intersectional look at how different people experience intimacy.

Target Audience: Young adults and anyone interested in diverse perspectives on bodies and relationships.
Why It's Unique: Hannah's openness about her own chronic illness and stoma adds a vital layer of disability representation to the sex-positive space.
Episode Highlight: "Sex and Disability with..." — An eye-opening conversation about accessibility and pleasure.
Relationships Interviews
#4 Best Honest Talk

The Manwhore Podcast

Hosted by Billy Procida

A sex-positive comedy podcast where Billy interviews former lovers, sex workers, and educators. It's an exploration of modern dating, vulnerability, and navigating sexuality as a cishet man.

Target Audience: Men looking for vulnerable male perspectives on sex, and anyone who enjoys comedy mixed with intimacy.
Why It's Unique: It breaks down toxic masculinity by embracing vulnerability and open conversations about past relationships.
Episode Highlight: "My Ex-Girlfriend's Perspective" — Billy interviews his exes in a radical display of honesty and accountability.
LGBTQ+ Comedy
#5 Best for Parents

Sex Positive Families

Hosted by Melissa Carnagey

Providing families with the resources they need to raise sexually healthy, informed, and empowered children. It's a game-changer for breaking generational cycles of shame.

Target Audience: Parents, caregivers, and educators wanting to approach sex ed confidently.
Why It's Unique: It specifically tackles the daunting task of talking to kids about bodies, boundaries, and sex without awkwardness.
Episode Highlight: "Talking About Consent with Toddlers" — Essential listening for teaching bodily autonomy from day one.
Education Parenting
#6 Best Real Stories

Sex Stories: Real People Talking About Sex

Hosted by Luna Robbie

Exactly what it sounds like: real, everyday people sharing their intimate stories anonymously. It's highly relatable, sometimes funny, sometimes heartbreaking, and always real.

Target Audience: Voyeurs and those who want to hear about real-life sexual experiences without the pornification.
Why It's Unique: The raw authenticity of anonymous confessions normalizes the wide spectrum of human desire.
Episode Highlight: "The Threesome That Saved Our Marriage" — A deeply honest look at how consensual non-monogamy can work.
Stories Authentic
#7 Best for Spiritual Sex

Orgasmic Enlightenment

Hosted by Kim Anami

Kim Anami connects sexual energy with life force energy. She explores how having better, deeper, more connected sex can literally transform the rest of your life.

Target Audience: Women and couples looking to combine spirituality with profound sexual awakening.
Why It's Unique: Introduces concepts like vaginal weightlifting and cervical orgasms in a deeply spiritual context.
Episode Highlight: "The Well-F*cked Woman" — Exploring how deep sexual fulfillment radiates into everyday confidence.
Pleasure Wellness
#8 Best for Couples

The Naked Connection

Hosted by Kirsten Trammell

Focused on helping couples navigate the tricky waters of long-term intimacy, communication, and keeping the spark alive when life gets in the way.

Target Audience: Long-term couples wanting to improve their emotional and physical connection.
Why It's Unique: Very practical, actionable advice that addresses the reality of busy, modern relationships.
Episode Highlight: "Navigating Mismatched Libidos" — Actionable strategies for couples with different sex drives.
Relationships Advice
#9 Best Gen-Z Vibe

Is it in Yet?

Hosted by The Tab

A fun, fast-paced podcast addressing the sexual queries and dilemmas of young people today. From hookup culture to navigating modern dating apps.

Target Audience: University students and young adults.
Why It's Unique: Captures the specific sexual zeitgeist of the younger generation perfectly.
Episode Highlight: "The Truth About Hookup Culture" — A candid discussion on casual sex and setting boundaries in your twenties.
Education Culture
#10 Best for Self-Discovery

Breaking Free Authentically

Hosted by Karine Bedard

Explores what it means to live and love authentically. Focuses heavily on breaking free from societal expectations around relationships and sexuality.

Target Audience: Anyone feeling trapped by traditional relationship milestones.
Why It's Unique: Deep dives into the psychology of why we desire what we desire.
Episode Highlight: "Deconstructing the Relationship Escalator" — Questioning the default path of marriage and kids.
Relationships Self-Help
#11 Best Clinical Approach

The Sex Positive Podcast

Hosted by Carrie Anne Hojnoski

A registered social worker and sex educator takes on everything from libido mismatch to sexual trauma, providing a gentle, clinically-informed perspective.

Target Audience: Individuals wanting trauma-informed, professional insight into their sexual health.
Why It's Unique: The perfect blend of clinical expertise and accessible, warm delivery.
Episode Highlight: "Healing from Sexual Trauma" — A gentle, expert-led episode on reclaiming your body and pleasure.
Education Therapy
#12 Best for ENM

Swinging for the Fences

Hosted by The Swing Nation

An honest, inside look at the swinging lifestyle. This podcast dispels the myths and gets real about the jealousy, the joy, and the logistics of ethical non-monogamy.

Target Audience: Couples curious about or currently navigating the swinging lifestyle.
Why It's Unique: Pulls back the curtain on a lifestyle that is highly stigmatized and misunderstood.
Episode Highlight: "Jealousy in the Lifestyle" — Real talk on handling the inevitable bumps in non-monogamy.
Kink Non-Monogamy
#13 Best Lifestyle Chat

Casual Swinger

Hosted by Mickey & Mallory

Mickey and Mallory share their personal journey through the swinging lifestyle, sharing stories, tips, and interviews with other couples in the community.

Target Audience: Anyone interested in the realities of open relationships from a lived-in perspective.
Why It's Unique: Very conversational and laid back—it feels like you're just chatting with friends who happen to swing.
Episode Highlight: "Our First Time at a Sex Club" — A funny, honest recount of stepping into the scene for the first time.
Kink Stories
#14 Best Erotic Memoir

The Curious Girl Diaries

Hosted by Leila

An audio diary of a woman exploring her sexuality outside of her marriage. It's an intimate, erotic, and deeply personal journey of sexual awakening.

Target Audience: Those who enjoy audio erotica and deeply personal narratives about female pleasure.
Why It's Unique: It blurs the line between podcast and erotic storytelling beautifully.
Episode Highlight: "The Stranger in the Bar" — A sensual exploration of a spontaneous encounter.
Stories Erotica
#15 Best Media Critique

Girls on Porn

Hosted by Headgum

Two women watch porn and review it. It's hilarious, critical, and opens up great conversations about ethical consumption, female pleasure, and the absurdity of mainstream adult content.

Target Audience: People who watch porn but wish it was better, or just enjoy feminist comedy.
Why It's Unique: It takes something usually consumed in private and turns it into a loud, funny, critical public conversation.
Episode Highlight: "Reviewing the 'Step-Sibling' Genre" — A hilarious breakdown of porn's weirdest mainstream obsession.
Pleasure Comedy

Understanding the Assessment Areas

Desire & Arousal

Exploring how you connect with your libido, understanding spontaneous vs. responsive desire, and recognizing your turn-ons and turn-offs.

Communication

Evaluating your comfort level in expressing boundaries, asking for what you want, and discussing intimacy openly with partners.

Body Image

Assessing how you feel in your skin during intimate moments. Feeling grounded and self-accepting can significantly enhance pleasure.

Relationship Satisfaction

Looking at the emotional connection, trust, and overall fulfillment within your intimate partnerships.

Physical Comfort & Dysfunction

Checking in on any pain, discomfort, or physical challenges during sex, which are common but often treatable.

Orgasm & Pleasure

Understanding your experiences with climax, prioritizing your pleasure, and unlearning the pressure to perform.

Overall Well-being

A holistic view of how your sexual health integrates with your emotional and mental wellness.

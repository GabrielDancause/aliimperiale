    Educational Resources

    Routine STI Testing

    Most experts recommend annual screening for sexually active adults. Those with multiple partners or engaging in higher-risk activities should consider testing every 3-6 months. Standard panels usually cover Chlamydia, Gonorrhea, Syphilis, and HIV.

    Barrier Methods

    External (male) and internal (female) condoms, as well as dental dams, are highly effective at preventing the transmission of fluid-borne STIs when used correctly and consistently. They also reduce the risk of skin-to-skin STIs like HPV and Herpes.

    PrEP & Preventive Care

    PrEP (Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis) is a daily medication that significantly reduces the risk of contracting HIV. Discuss with a healthcare provider to see if PrEP, DoxyPEP, or vaccines (like HPV and Hepatitis B) are right for you.

    Treatment Options

    Many STIs, including Chlamydia, Gonorrhea, and Syphilis, are highly curable with antibiotics. Viral STIs like Herpes and HIV are treatable and highly manageable with modern medication, allowing individuals to live healthy lives and prevent transmission.

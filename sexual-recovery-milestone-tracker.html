Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure and private?

Yes, absolutely. This tracker operates entirely in your browser using local storage. No data is sent to our servers or anyone else. Your milestones, journal entries, and progress remain strictly on your device.

How do I use the milestone tracker?

Start by setting realistic goals for your emotional, physical, communication, and intimacy recovery. Check off milestones as you achieve them, use the journal space for reflections, and watch your progress visualization grow.

What if I feel triggered while using the tracker?

Healing is not linear, and it's okay to take breaks. If you feel overwhelmed or triggered, step away. We have provided crisis support contacts and professional resources at the bottom of the page if you need immediate assistance.

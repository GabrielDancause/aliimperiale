Why You Should Track Your Cycle (And Your Feels)

Look, we're complicated, magical creatures. Our bodies aren't machines that operate at 100% capacity every single day, and that's totally okay. Tracking your cycle, your energy, and your mood isn't about being perfectly organized—it's about gathering data on you.

The Magic of Patterns

When you log how you feel every day, you start noticing trends. Maybe you always get a little teary-eyed three days before your period. Maybe your sex drive skyrockets mid-month when you're ovulating. Once you spot these patterns, you stop judging yourself for "random" moods and start anticipating what your body needs. It's the ultimate self-care hack.

What to Look Out For

Keep an eye out for extreme shifts. If your period pain suddenly goes from "annoying" to "I need to stay in bed all day," or if your energy levels drop off a cliff and stay there for weeks, that's your body waving a red flag. Tracking gives you the receipts you need when you go to the doctor, so you don't just have to say "I feel weird" — you can show them exactly when and how.

When to See a Doctor

This tool is for your personal insight and is definitely not medical advice. You should book an appointment with a healthcare professional if you experience: bleeding between periods, pain during sex, consistently irregular cycles, abnormally heavy bleeding, or severe pain that regular over-the-counter meds can't touch. You know your body best—if something feels off, get it checked out!

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is it important to track my sexual health?
Tracking your sexual health, including periods, mood, energy levels, and symptoms, helps you understand your body's unique patterns. It empowers you to anticipate changes, identify potential issues early, and have more informed conversations with healthcare providers.
What should I track for my sexual wellness?
It's beneficial to track menstruation dates, physical symptoms like cramps or bloating, mood fluctuations, energy levels, and even changes in libido. Tracking these can reveal how your cycle affects your overall well-being.
When should I see a doctor about my period or symptoms?
You should consult a healthcare professional if you experience sudden changes in your cycle, abnormally heavy bleeding, severe pain that disrupts daily activities, bleeding between periods, or if you have concerns about unusual discharge or persistent mood changes.
Is my data safe in this tracker?
Yes. This tracker is privacy-focused. All your logged data is stored locally on your device (in your browser's local storage). No information is sent to or stored on any external servers.

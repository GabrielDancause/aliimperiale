Your Relationship Timeline

Why Track Relationship Milestones?

I absolutely love the idea of creating a visual map of a relationship. It's so easy to get caught up in the day-to-day routine and forget just how far you've come together. A relationship timeline isn't just about marking dates on a calendar; it's about honoring the journey, celebrating the wins, and acknowledging the challenges you've overcome hand-in-hand.

The Power of Shared Memories

When we take the time to document our milestones—whether it's the nervous excitement of a first date, the chaotic joy of moving in together, or the vulnerability of navigating a tough transition—we're actually strengthening our bond. Research shows that reminiscing about positive shared experiences can boost relationship satisfaction and foster a deeper sense of connection.

Planning for the Future

This tool isn't just for looking backward; it's for dreaming forward. By adding "Future Goals" to your timeline, you and your partner can align on what you're working toward. Whether it's planning a dream vacation, buying a house, or focusing on personal growth, putting it on the timeline makes it feel real and achievable.

Relationship Health Check-ins

You'll notice an option to include a "Relationship Health Check-in" for certain milestones. Relationships are dynamic, and checking in on how you're both feeling during major life events (like moving, changing jobs, or hitting a big anniversary) is a proactive way to ensure you're staying connected and communicating openly.

Remember, your timeline is beautifully unique. There's no "right" pace or required order for milestones. Celebrate your story exactly as it unfolds!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my relationship data private?

Absolutely. Your privacy is a top priority. This tool runs entirely in your browser using local storage. None of your data, text, or uploaded photos are ever sent to a server, tracked, or saved externally. It lives purely on your specific device.

Can I add future events or goals?

Yes! You can select a future date to add upcoming goals, planned trips, or relationship aspirations to your timeline. It's a great way to visualize what you're building toward together.

How do I share my timeline with my partner?

You can use the "Share Link" button to generate a shareable URL containing your timeline data, or you can use the "Export Data" button to save a copy for your records. *Note: If you attached local photos, they will not transfer via the simple share link due to browser security restrictions, but text data will!*

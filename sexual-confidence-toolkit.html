Understanding Sexual Confidence

Let's clear something up right away: there's a huge difference between sexual confidence and sexual performance.

Confidence vs. Performance

  • Sexual Performance: Focuses on the external. It's about trying to impress a partner, worrying about how you look, and treating sex like a routine to be perfectly executed.
  • Sexual Confidence: Focuses on the internal experience. It's about being present in your body, focusing on pleasure, laughing when things get awkward, and trusting your worth.

Cultural Influences on Sexual Self-Worth

We are bombarded with heavily edited, unrealistic images of what a "sexy" body is supposed to look like. Diet culture and the beauty industry profit off your insecurity. Reclaiming your body image is a radical act of self-love.

Overcoming Sexual Shame and Anxiety

None of us are born feeling insecure about our bodies or our desires. Those insecurities are learned. Negative experiences, shame-based sex education, or trauma can leave lasting scars. Acknowledge what's been tearing your confidence down. Saying, "I'm feeling a little nervous right now," takes the power away from the anxiety.

Building Positive Sexual Identity

Building sexual confidence starts with building sexual self-awareness. Taking the time to explore your own body, without the pressure of a partner, teaches you how you like to be touched. Define your boundaries and interests.

Maintaining Confidence in Relationships

Your sexual confidence will look different depending on the context. You might feel incredibly confident with a long-term partner but nervous on a first date. The goal is to develop an internal baseline of self-worth that you carry with you into any situation. Practice assertiveness outside the bedroom to build muscle memory for inside the bedroom.
